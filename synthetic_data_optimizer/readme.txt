Here we have code (acknowledgement: code has been developed with the assistance of Gemini 3.1.Pro, 2026)
and slides regarding tuning the parameters of the synthetic 
generator methods for multi-objective optimization of risk-utility (many measures for both), while 
considering and using the uncertainty of estimates. We include differential privacy and spatially adapting 
the methods in order to account for the impact of the geospatial dimensions. 
Three broad categories of methods are optimised: 
NN-based, joint probability like Bayesian models and Bayesian Netowrks, tree-based.
